Privacy Policy

Effective 23 November 2026. This policy explains how the Yarn Messages app for Android ("Yarn", package app.yarn.messages) handles your information.

The short version

  • Your messages stay on your phone. Yarn has no servers of its own and does not collect, sell or share your data.
  • Yarn contains no advertising and no analytics or tracking.
  • If you turn on Google Drive backup, an encrypted backup is saved to a private app folder in your own Google Drive. Only Yarn on your devices can read it.
  • Yarn is free. Yarn Pro is bought through Google Play, and Google handles all payment details; Yarn never sees them.
  • The only message text that ever leaves your phone is one you choose to translate online, or a backup you turn on.
  • A logo request you confirm is sent as a text message from your phone to Yarn's developer.

Information Yarn uses on your phone

  • SMS and MMS messages. As your default messaging app, Yarn reads, sends, receives and stores messages so you can use it to message. They are kept in an encrypted database on your phone, protected by a key held in Android's secure keystore.
  • Contacts. Used to show names and photos and to recognise people you know. Contacts are not uploaded anywhere.
  • Phone state and SIM information. Used to send from the right SIM and to work with dual-SIM phones.
  • Notifications. Used to alert you to new messages.

Smart features

Sorting, spam and scam detection, code detection, summaries and suggested replies run on your phone. Yarn uses on-device technology including Google ML Kit and, on supported phones, Gemini Nano. Message text is processed on the device and is not sent to Yarn. Some of these components may download their on-device models from Google the first time they are used. When you correct a category or mark spam, Yarn learns locally; you can erase what it has learned in Settings.

Online translation. Yarn translates on your phone whenever it can. For Hinglish and languages your phone can't translate by itself, and only while Translate online when needed is on (it is by default), the one message you tap Translate on is sent to Google Translate, and nothing else. Turn this off in Settings → Smart features. Google's privacy policy covers its use of that text.

Business names and logos are built into the app. Yarn matches a sender's ID against its own list on your phone and never looks senders up online.

Google Drive backup (optional)

If you choose to sign in with Google in Settings → Backup & restore, Yarn requests:

  • Google Drive app data (drive.appdata): to create, read and delete Yarn's own backup files in a hidden app folder of your Google Drive. Yarn cannot see any other files in your Drive.
  • Your email address: to show which Google account backups go to.

You can turn backup on during setup or later. Backups contain your messages, attachments (if you choose), each chat's settings and unsent draft, your profile name and photo, your Yarn settings and language, the recycle bin, and what Yarn has learned. They are encrypted (AES-256) before upload. The encryption key is stored in the same private app folder so you can restore by signing in to the same Google account on a new phone. Only the latest backup is kept. Backups count towards your own Google storage. Your use of Google Drive is also covered by Google's privacy policy.

Yarn's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Yarn uses this access only to back up and restore your messages at your request, and never for advertising.

Yarn Pro and purchases

Yarn is free to use. Yarn Pro adds more (smart tabs, spam protection, business logos, automatic backup and more). Every new install gets Yarn Pro free for 30 days; after that Yarn keeps working for free, and Pro can be bought through Google Play Billing as a yearly subscription or once for life (people who helped test Yarn may get Pro for life through a Google Play promo code). Google Play handles payment, renewal, refunds and your payment details under Google's terms and privacy policy. Yarn only asks Google Play, on your phone, whether your Google account owns Yarn Pro, and keeps that answer on your phone.

The trial's start date. So that reinstalling Yarn doesn't start a new trial, Yarn remembers the date your trial began, and nothing else, in three places: on your phone; in Google's Block Store, which Android keeps for Yarn across reinstalls and restores with your Google backup; and, if you turn on Google Drive backup, in a small file (yarn-pro-trial) in Yarn's private app folder in your Drive. None of these is sent to Yarn. Manage or cancel the subscription anytime in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions.

Feedback

If you choose Send feedback, Yarn opens WhatsApp or your email app with a note written for you to send. It includes the Yarn version, Android version and phone model, and by email Yarn's error log, which holds no message content. Nothing is sent unless you send it, and whatever you send is then handled by WhatsApp or your email provider.

Logo requests

With Yarn Pro, when a business has no logo in Yarn you can ask for one (long-press its picture in the inbox, or Request a logo in the chat's menu). After you confirm, Yarn sends one text message from your SIM to Yarn's developer containing only the business's name, its sender ID (for example CP-APLPHR-S) and Yarn's version number, never your messages. Like any text message, it shows the developer your phone number, which is used only to read the request and is never shared. Normal SMS charges from your carrier may apply. Each business is requested once.

Updates

Yarn uses Google Play's in-app update service to tell you when a new version is available. This is handled by Google Play on your device.

Deleting your data

  • Uninstalling Yarn deletes its encrypted database from your phone. Messages also remain in Android's system message store unless you delete them.
  • To stop backups, choose Turn off Google backup in Yarn. To delete existing backups, open Google Drive on the web → Settings → Manage apps → Yarn → Delete hidden app data, or remove Yarn's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Children

Yarn is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.
