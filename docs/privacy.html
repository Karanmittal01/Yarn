Privacy Policy

Effective 1 October 2026. This policy explains how the Yarn Messages app for Android ("Yarn", package app.yarn.messages) handles your information.

The short version

  • Your messages stay on your phone. Yarn has no servers of its own and does not collect, sell or share your data.
  • Yarn contains no advertising and no analytics or tracking.
  • If you turn on Google Drive backup, an encrypted backup is saved to a private app folder in your own Google Drive. Only Yarn on your devices can read it.

Information Yarn uses on your phone

  • SMS and MMS messages. As your default messaging app, Yarn reads, sends, receives and stores messages so you can use it to message. They are kept in an encrypted database on your phone, protected by a key held in Android's secure keystore.
  • Contacts. Used to show names and photos and to recognise people you know. Contacts are not uploaded anywhere.
  • Phone state and SIM information. Used to send from the right SIM and to work with dual-SIM phones.
  • Notifications. Used to alert you to new messages.

Smart features

Sorting, spam and scam detection, code detection, summaries and suggested replies run on your phone. Yarn uses on-device technology including Google ML Kit and, on supported phones, Gemini Nano. Message text is processed on the device and is not sent to Yarn. Some of these components may download their on-device models from Google the first time they are used. When you correct a category or mark spam, Yarn learns locally; you can erase what it has learned in Settings.

Google Drive backup (optional)

If you choose to sign in with Google in Settings → Backup & restore, Yarn requests:

  • Google Drive app data (drive.appdata): to create, read and delete Yarn's own backup files in a hidden app folder of your Google Drive. Yarn cannot see any other files in your Drive.
  • Your email address: to show which Google account backups go to.

Backups contain your messages, attachments (if you choose), conversation settings and what Yarn has learned. They are encrypted (AES-256) before upload. The encryption key is stored in the same private app folder so you can restore by signing in to the same Google account on a new phone. Only the latest backup is kept. Backups count towards your own Google storage. Your use of Google Drive is also covered by Google's privacy policy.

Yarn's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Yarn uses this access only to back up and restore your messages at your request, and never for advertising.

Updates

Yarn uses Google Play's in-app update service to tell you when a new version is available. This is handled by Google Play on your device.

Deleting your data

  • Uninstalling Yarn deletes its encrypted database from your phone. Messages also remain in Android's system message store unless you delete them.
  • To stop backups, choose Turn off Google backup in Yarn. To delete existing backups, open Google Drive on the web → Settings → Manage apps → Yarn → Delete hidden app data, or remove Yarn's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Children

Yarn is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Contact the developer using the email address shown on Yarn's Google Play listing.
