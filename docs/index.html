Yarn Messages

Yarn is a private, smart SMS and MMS app for Android. It sorts your messages into Personal, OTP, Transactions, Shopping, Updates and Offers, filters spam and scams, and keeps everything on your phone.

Private by design. Your messages are stored in an encrypted database on your phone. Sorting and smart features run on your phone. Yarn has no servers and no analytics.

Optional Google Drive backup. If you choose, Yarn saves an encrypted backup to a private folder in your own Google Drive so you can restore on a new phone.
